Document Contents

The Cadence FRD documents requirements across four integrated HR workflow modules. Each module follows the full BABOK v3 structure: problem statement, objectives with success indicators, stakeholder map, scope boundaries with rationale, functional requirements (MoSCoW-prioritized), and non-functional requirements with rationale paragraphs.

The document establishes the traceability chain from business objectives through to implementation, and includes the data model and cross-workflow dependency map showing how WF1 headcount data feeds WF2 compensation eligibility and how WF4 performance ratings flow into WF3 attrition risk scoring.

  • Section 1 Executive Summary & Business Problem
  • Section 2 Stakeholder Map & Personas
  • Section 3 Portfolio Objectives (OBJ-01 to OBJ-08)
  • Section 4 WF1: Headcount Reporting (WF1-FR-001 to WF1-FR-017)
  • Section 5 WF4: Performance Management (WF4-FR-001 to WF4-FR-016)
  • Section 6 WF2: Compensation Review (WF2-FR-001 to WF2-FR-015)
  • Section 7 WF3: Attrition Risk Scoring (WF3-FR-001 to WF3-FR-011)
  • Section 8 Non-Functional Requirements
  • Section 9 Out-of-Scope Items & Future Phases
  • Section 10 Traceability Matrix

The full FRD is available as a Word document on GitHub. It includes all functional requirements tables, MoSCoW prioritization, NFR rationale paragraphs, and the cross-workflow traceability matrix.
