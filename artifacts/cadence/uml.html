WF1 — Headcount Reporting

headcount_snapshots, pipeline_run_log — Central employee record anchor. CSV upload pipeline with idempotent upsert. Feeds WF2 and WF3 downstream.

WF2 — Compensation Review

salary_bands, merit_cycles, merit_eligibility, merit_recommendations — Full merit cycle lifecycle. Eligibility engine auto-determines from WF1 data. Band compliance enforced at recommendation.

WF4 — Performance Management

performance_reviews, one_on_ones, monthly_checkins — Weekly 1:1 capture, monthly check-ins with employee acknowledgement, and quarterly formal reviews with HR approval gate.

WF3 — Attrition Risk Scoring

attrition_risk_scores, risk_factor_config, retention_actions — Composite 0-100 risk score aggregating signals from WF1, WF2, and WF4. Configurable weights via risk_factor_config.

Cross-workflow data feeds: WF1 supplies headcount and salary data to WF2 (eligibility) and WF3 (compa-ratio signal). WF4 supplies rating trends and check-in frequency to both WF2 (performance modifier) and WF3 (sentiment and engagement signals). WF2 supplies merit history to WF3 (time-since-merit signal). All feeds are read-only lookups — no circular dependencies.
