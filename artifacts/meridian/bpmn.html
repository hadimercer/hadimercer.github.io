Diagram 1 — Workstream Lifecycle

Main process: Owner creates a workstream, completes the 9-question wizard, then manages ongoing activity across three parallel lanes. System recalculates RAG scores on every write from the contributor lane.

Main Process

Three-lane BPMN flow across Owner, Contributor/Viewer, and System. Parallel split after wizard completion triggers both collaborative activity and RAG initialisation.

Diagram 2 — Invite & Role Promotion Sub-Process

Sub-process covering new user onboarding via invite link, automatic Viewer role assignment, and optional promotion to Contributor by the workspace Owner.
