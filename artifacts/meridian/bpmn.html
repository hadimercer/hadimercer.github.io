Diagram 1: Workstream Lifecycle

Main process: Owner creates a workstream, completes the 9-question wizard, then manages ongoing activity across three parallel lanes. System recalculates RAG scores on every write from the contributor lane.

Main Process

Three-lane BPMN flow across Owner, Contributor/Viewer, and System. Parallel split after wizard completion triggers both collaborative activity and RAG initialization.

flowchart TD subgraph OWNER["Owner"] S([Start]) --> Login["Sign Up / Log In"] Login --> CreateWS["Create Workstream"] CreateWS --> Wizard["Complete 9-Question Wizard"] Wizard --> PSplit{Parallel Split} PSplit --> PostUpdates["Post to Updates Tab"] PostUpdates --> WorkComplete{Work Complete?} WorkComplete -->|Yes| Archive["Archive Workstream"] Archive --> E([End]) WorkComplete -->|"No, continue managing"| PostUpdates end subgraph CONTRIBUTOR["Contributor / Viewer"] InviteJoin(["Joins via Invite Link"]) --> AddComments["Add Comments & Notes"] AddComments --> UpdateData["Update Milestones, Spend & Blockers"] end subgraph SYSTEM["System"] StoreConfig["Store Wizard Config"] InitRAG["Initialize RAG Score"] StoreConfig --> InitRAG RecalcRAG["Recalculate RAG Score"] InitRAG --> RecalcRAG CheckStale["Check Staleness"] RecalcRAG --> CheckStale end Wizard -.->|config| StoreConfig PSplit -.-> InviteJoin PSplit -.-> InitRAG UpdateData -.->|triggers| RecalcRAG CheckStale -.-> WorkComplete

Diagram 2: Invite & Role Promotion Sub-Process

Sub-process covering new user onboarding via invite link, automatic Viewer role assignment, and optional promotion to Contributor by the workspace Owner.

Invite & Role Promotion

Three-lane flow across Owner, New User, and System. Auto-assign on link click; Owner promotes after reviewing Team tab.
