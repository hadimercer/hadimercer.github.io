01The Business Problem

IT service organisations operate on intuition and surface-level averages. Incident resolution data exists in ticketing systems, but it is rarely interrogated beyond basic volume and SLA metrics.

"Leaders and operators often work from manual exports and surface-level averages, leaving them unable to understand how incidents actually move through the organisation, where time is lost, or what drives escalations and reopens."

Three critical blindspots drive incident management inefficiency:

Flow visibility: What are the most common incident paths? Which variants consume the most time? Where are the bottlenecks in the process?

Handoff friction: How many teams touch each incident? Where do handoffs create delay? Which transitions are most costly?

Quality signals: What drives escalations? Why do incidents reopen? What correlates with poor customer satisfaction outcomes?

04Scope Boundaries

IncidentOps is an analytics tool only — it does not update tickets, integrate with live ticketing systems, or drive automated actions. The system relies on CSV export input, not live API connections. All findings are descriptive and based on historical patterns; process mining outputs are not causal proof.

Designed for portfolio and local execution — not enterprise multi-user deployment. The value proposition is reproducibility, analytical clarity, and business framing of incident patterns.
